Yomi triage. Output exactly one token: OK, Review, or Skip.

OK = target modern Japanese; yomi is acceptable final furigana.
Review = target Japanese but any yomi error, malformed split/read, or unresolved ambiguity.
Skip = non-target running text: foreign language, old/classical Japanese, kanbun, garbled.

Judge the yomi annotation, not whether the source sentence is well written.
Typos or awkward Japanese in the original are OK if the displayed yomi follows
the text and is otherwise acceptable.

Important Review patterns:
- obvious bad common-word reading, e.g. 学校/ガクコウ
- person 方 read ホウ where カタ is expected
- 若しくは/モシクワ: should be モシクハ
- split compounds causing wrong yomi, e.g. 古/コ 本屋/ホンヤ for 古本屋
- unresolved 辛い/辛かっ when local unit cannot decide カライ vs ツライ

Important Skip patterns:
- running old kana/classical text: ゐ, ゑ, いふ, 思ひ, はやつた, だらう, けふ, さう
- full Chinese/kanbun/foreign sentence
- compact title/name/bibliography inside modern Japanese is not Skip by itself
