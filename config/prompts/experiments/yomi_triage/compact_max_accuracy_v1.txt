Yomi triage for a Japanese reading corpus.
Return exactly one token, no explanation: OK, Review, or Skip.

The yomi display is compact:
- Tokens with `/reading` are the only places whose reading needs inspection.
- Bare tokens intentionally omit readings for kana, symbols, numbers, and
  non-kanji/non-Latin surfaces.
- Bare kana after a kanji token may complete the word or inflection. This is
  normal. For example `戻っ/モドッ て`, `調べ/シラベ て`,
  `確かめ/タシカメ ましょう`, `高く/タカク なり`, and `減っ/ヘッ て`
  are OK if the combined reading follows the text.
- Bare numbers are intentional. Number pronunciation is handled elsewhere.
  Do not Review just because `1984`, `45`, `2`, `７０`, `%`, or `.` is bare.

Labels:

OK = target modern Japanese and the yomi can be accepted at this stage.
Review = target Japanese, but at least one yomi pair must not be auto-accepted.
Skip = non-target running text: old/classical Japanese, kanbun, Chinese,
foreign-language text, or badly garbled text.

Decision order:
1. If the text is mostly non-target running text, choose Skip.
2. Otherwise scan the displayed `/reading` pairs in context.
3. If any important pair is wrong, malformed, or contextually unresolved,
   choose Review.
4. Otherwise choose OK.

Judge the yomi annotation, not prose quality. Web noise, casual wording,
punctuation oddities, source-text typos, and fine morphological segmentation are
OK if the readings follow the text.
Do not use ジ/ヂ spelling preferences as the main reason for Review.

Choose Review for:
- obvious wrong common-word readings even inside long sentences: 学校/ガクコウ,
  今日/コンニチ when context means キョウ
- standalone 人 read ジン where normal reading is ヒト
- malformed 人 compounds caused by bad yomi, e.g. 一人一人/ジンリヒトリ
- standalone 時 read ジ where normal reading is トキ
- 出る/出て/出た as 出/シュツ instead of デ
- person-sense 方 read ホウ where カタ is expected
- bad split/read pairs: 古/コ 本屋/ホンヤ for 古本屋
- 若しくは/モシクワ, where furigana should be モシクハ
- unresolved local ambiguity that should stay reviewable, e.g. 辛い/辛かっ
  when the unit itself does not decide カライ vs ツライ
- deictic ambiguity around 辛い, such as "that thing/that event was 辛い";
  even if ツライ is plausible, keep Review when the unit does not fully decide

Choose Skip for:
- running old kana/classical style: 思ふ, いふ, 云ふ, 云へる, さう,
  はやつた, だらう, ゐる, けふ
- long old-kanji prose: 爲, 會, 處, 當, 實, 體, 圖, 邊, 醫, 國 when used
  as running text
- kanbun or Chinese sentence
- foreign-language sentence
- spam-like mixed Chinese/foreign-language credential or sales text embedded in
  Japanese

Usually OK, not Review by itself:
- 頂き/イタダキ for 頂く, 皆/ミナ さん, 私/ワタクシ, 日本/ニッポン
- normal split inflections such as 願い/ネガイ ます, 戻っ/モドッ て,
  し, さ, れ, た, ます, です, でしょう
- numeric expressions such as `45 秒/ビョウ`, `2 回/カイ`, `７０ ％`,
  `1984 年/ネン`

Do not choose Skip for:
- a modern Japanese sentence that only contains a short old-character title,
  proper name, bibliographic phrase, proverb, or quoted expression.
