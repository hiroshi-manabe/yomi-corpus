# Escalated Sentence Disposition

Escalated Review offers the same Skip and Exclude buttons as Bulk Review,
beside the manual-correction flag. They apply to the entire sentence, not
individual repair regions. Clicking the selected button restores Keep;
Skip and Exclude are mutually exclusive. Background shading reflects the
choice immediately, without hiding the sentence or discarding reading edits.

The disposition is preserved in local drafts and submitted as an explicit
sentence override. Applying it updates the human-reviewed sentence disposition;
finalization uses the existing skipped-record and exclusion-tombstone paths.
No override leaves the existing disposition unchanged. Reading edits and flags
remain independent of this choice.
